Started in 1954, still family run

John Elmer Carlson opened Carlson's Auto Body in April 1954, following his own father, who had started a body shop in West Chester back in the 1920s. The original shop was on Matlack Street in West Chester. After the Lima Fire Company moved into a new building in 1954, John took over the vacancy and opened here on West Baltimore Pike.

The next two generations show up every day

John ran the shop mostly on his own for twenty years. In the early 1970s his son Eric joined full time after a few summers of apprenticeship. Eric is now in his 38th year with the company, still runs the paint department, and serves as President. In the early 1980s his brother Gunnar joined too, learning assembly work from their father and Eric. Gunnar is Secretary/Treasurer and handles a lot of the customer side of the business.
