Third generation, same shop, same name on the door

Call Us for an Appointment

Carlson's Auto Body has been fixing cars on West Baltimore Pike since 1954. John Elmer Carlson started it. His sons Eric and Gunnar run it now. We still do our own paint work, still deal straight with insurance, and still give free estimates.

What happens under the lights

This is our paint department, where Eric has spent 38 years learning every inch of it. Every car that comes through gets prepped, primed, and sprayed with the DuPont Quality Paint System before it goes back out on the road looking the way it did before the accident.

Your car, your choice of shop

In Pennsylvania, you choose where your car gets repaired. Not your insurance company. We work with all insurance companies and can help with deductible assistance, so the process is less of a headache for you.
